The text in Sticky Buns of SPLORR!! is drawn with the DINOBYTE bitmap font.

DINOBYTE bitmap font by mby, https://mby.itch.io/dinobyte
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The glyph sheet (dbyte_1x.png) is used unmodified. The game packs the sheet into
font_data.odin and tints each glyph with its own colours at draw time; the
shapes are not changed.
